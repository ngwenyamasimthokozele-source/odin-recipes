Beef stew recipe

Beef stew

This hearty beef stew combines tender beef, potatoes, carrots and peas in a rich, flavourful gravy. It is a warm and filling meal, perfect for cold days.

Serve it with fresh bread or rice to soak up the delicious gravy.

  • Ingredients:
    • 2 lbs beef stew meat, cut into cubes
    • 4 cups beef broth
    • 4 medium potatoes, peeled and diced
    • 3 carrots, peeled and sliced
    • 1 onion, chopped
    • 2 cloves garlic, minced
    • 1 cup frozen peas
    • 2 tbsp tomato paste
    • 1 tsp dried thyme
    • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Instructions:
    • In a large pot, brown the beef over medium heat.
    • Add the onion and garlic, cooking until softened.
    • Add the beef broth, tomato paste, thyme, salt, and pepper. Bring to a boil.
    • Add the potatoes and carrots. Reduce heat and simmer for 1-2 hours until the beef is tender.
    • Add the frozen peas in the last 10 minutes of cooking.
    • Serve hot with bread or rice.
